# Last Signal: research notes (Nov 2026)

Sources: r/projectzomboid, r/StateOfDecay and r/LastDayonEarthGame threads, Dawn of Zombies store reviews, the Diablo 3 itemization blog, Diablo 4 S4 coverage (Dexerto, GameSpot), the Gamasutra UX analysis of This War of Mine, the PC Gamer TWoM review, and the Pocket Gamer review of Into the Dead 2.

## What players love
- **The loot, organise, loot loop.** Many say the best part of PZ is going on dicey loot trips and then having cozy time back at base, building up a dragon's hoard (PZ "best part" thread).
- **A base that gets better.** Players love turning a house into a haven (PZ, SoD2).
- **Scarcity and tough choices.** Limited carry space makes you think about what to take, like "a shovel or a gun?" Safer places give less loot. Some people want to trade, not fight (TWoM).
- **Stealth tension.** Sound cues, slow and quiet versus fast and loud, and seeing the threat before you engage (TWoM).
- **Variety from run to run.** Random survivors and perks, legacy rewards, and seeing new things on the 6th run (SoD2).
- **Loot that feels special.** When a legendary drops, the player should think "how awesome is it?", not "is it any good?" Fewer junk drops make each find matter more (D3 blog).
- **Easy inventory management.** Players want Salvage/Sell All, junk and favourite flags, and upgrades highlighted at a glance. Bulk salvage must never eat legendaries or favourites, and it needs a confirm step (D4 S4 backlash).

## What players hate
- **Energy timers, paywalls, and gear that breaks too fast.** These come up constantly for LDoE and Dawn of Zombies.
- **Losing everything on death.** Dawn of Zombies is praised because you don't.
- **Constant full-inventory micro-management** (LDoE, D4).
- **Unfair or unclear deaths.** SoD players accept permadeath but hate bugs or hidden rules that kill a character.
- **Survival chores with a clunky UI** (PZ criticism).

## Applied to Last Signal
1. **One-tap scrap.** Clear downgrades are auto-tagged JUNK. Scrap All Junk asks for a confirm and never touches favourites, equipped gear, or anything Epic or better. Multi-select is available. Scrap turns into parts used at the workbench.
2. **Workbench.** Upgrade a gear piece's rarity, and craft bolts, bandages, lockpicks and molotovs.
3. **Wandering trader.** She buys loot for batteries and sells medicine, ammo, tools and the location of a radio part. This is the "trade, don't fight" option.
4. **Safehouse.** Your suburbs house can be upgraded with barricades, a bed, a garden and a radio. Resting there is safe; resting elsewhere is risky.
5. **Loot that matters.** Rarity is capped per zone, so legendaries are rare and strong. Consumables no longer roll rarity. There is less junk, plus scrap bundles. The crossbow uses bolts.
6. **Honest danger.** Labels are based on your current gear. Noise-drawn spawns are capped. Threat grows over the days, and a rescue deadline adds midgame pressure without any timers or paywalls.
7. **Fair deaths.** The game saves after every action and the reload exploits are closed. Kill-chance text is clear.
8. **Meta progression.** Journal points unlock starting kits. No energy system and no monetisation, ever.
